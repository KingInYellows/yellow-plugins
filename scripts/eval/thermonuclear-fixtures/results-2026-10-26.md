## Cross-host smoke

and yellow-review with `--plugin-dir` and dispatched
`yellow-review:review:thermonuclear-reviewer` through the Agent tool. The
subagent confirmed the `yellow-thermonuclear-review` skill was preloaded, quoted
the pinned commit `6e3d2ea…`, both blob SHAs and the MIT notice verbatim, and
listed only Read, Grep and Glob. Two things came up on the way, neither a defect
in the persona:

- yellow-review does not load from `--plugin-dir` without yellow-linear, even
  though its manifest marks that dependency `"optional": true`. Claude Code
  2.1.310 logs "Dependency yellow-linear is not installed" and skips the plugin.
- `claude --agent yellow-review:review:thermonuclear-reviewer` runs the persona
  as the main-thread agent without its `skills:` preload; the persona then says
  the rubric is missing. `/review:pr` dispatches through the Agent tool, where
  the preload works.
